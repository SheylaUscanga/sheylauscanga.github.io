Selected Work

Current Research

AI Travel Planners and Epistemic Agency

In progress

I am examining how AI-generated travel recommendations can carry assumptions across contexts and shape what users perceive as available, realistic, or possible. The project focuses on travel planning in Mexico as a way of examining how seemingly reasonable recommendations can overlook local specificities concerning mobility, infrastructure, security and everyday life.

Undergraduate Thesis

Configuring AI Decision-Making Capacity in the European Union

In progress

This undergraduate thesis examines how the European Union configures the decision-making capacity of artificial intelligence through the ecosystem of regulations developed between 2016 and 2024. It traces how legal and institutional arrangements define, delimit and organize the field of action available to AI systems.

Research Seminar

Automation, Subjectivity & Critical Theory

In progress

This research explores the relationship between automation, artificial intelligence, subjectivity and power through critical theory, political economy, psychoanalysis and the Frankfurt School. The project asks how technological systems participate in the organization of social relations and forms of subjectivity.

Research Focus

AI Regulation and Institutional Configuration in the European Union

Research focus

This work examines AI regulation as an institutional process, focusing on how European Union institutions, Member State priorities, regulatory instruments and emerging structures contribute to the configuration of the field in which artificial intelligence develops and operates.

COIL · Mexico & Argentina

Cross-Border Cyber Defense

Completed project

This comparative project examined national cybersecurity strategies in Mexico and Argentina, considering the institutional and geopolitical contexts in which cyber defense is organized. The project approached cybersecurity as a cross-border issue shaped by different national strategies and institutional configurations.
