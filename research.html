Artificial Intelligence ∩ Epistemic Agency

AI Travel Planners and Epistemic Agency

Status: Current research interest

This project examines how AI travel planners can shape users' epistemic agency: their ability to understand their circumstances, evaluate alternatives, and decide what is possible for them. It asks when recommendations become contextually wrong even when they are factually accurate, particularly when systems rely on assumptions about infrastructure, security, mobility, leisure, and everyday life in Mexico.

Artificial Intelligence ∩ Regulation & Law

Configuring AI Decision-Making Capacity in the European Union

Status: Undergraduate thesis · BUAP · 2016–2024

My undergraduate thesis examines how the decision-making capacity of artificial intelligence is configured through the European Union's regulatory ecosystem. It traces the development of relevant rules and institutional arrangements from data protection and biometric provisions to the AI Act, focusing on how legal and regulatory frameworks define, delimit, and organize the field of action available to AI systems.

Epistemic Agency ∩ Institutions & Power

Automation, Subjectivity & Critical Theory

Status: Research seminar

This line of work considers AI through questions of subjectivity, knowledge, and power. Drawing on critical theory, political economy, psychoanalysis, and the Frankfurt School, it explores how technological systems participate in shaping the conditions through which people understand themselves, interpret their surroundings, and act within institutional structures.

Regulation & Law ∩ Institutions & Power

AI Regulation and Institutional Configuration in the European Union

Status: Research focus

This research focus approaches AI regulation as an institutional process. It considers the interaction between European Union institutions, Member State priorities, regulatory instruments, and emerging structures for AI oversight, with attention to how different actors participate in defining the boundaries within which AI systems are developed and deployed.

Institutions & Power ∩ International Affairs

Cross-Border Cyber Defense

Status: COIL Mexico–Argentina

A comparative project examining national cybersecurity strategies in Mexico and Argentina, with attention to the institutional and geopolitical contexts in which cyber defense policies are developed. The project considers how national approaches to cybersecurity are shaped by broader questions of international relations and state capacity.
