About

I am a BSc student in International Relations at Benemérita Universidad Autónoma de Puebla (BUAP), interested in the relationship between international affairs, institutions, law and emerging technologies.

My work brings together questions about how institutions organize knowledge, how technologies shape decision-making, and how these processes operate across different social and political contexts.

I am particularly interested in artificial intelligence, AI regulation, epistemic agency, international law, institutions and power, human mobility, and Latin America.
