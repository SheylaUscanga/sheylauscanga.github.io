Sheyla Uscanga Vazquez

International Relations · Artificial Intelligence · International Law

I am an International Relations student interested in artificial intelligence, international law, and the institutional and political contexts that shape how systems of decision-making are developed and deployed.

My work brings together questions of AI governance and regulation with broader interests in institutions, international affairs, human mobility, and Latin America. I am particularly interested in what happens when formal systems encounter the social and political contexts in which they operate.

My current research examines how the decision-making capacity of artificial intelligence is configured through the European Union's regulatory ecosystem. Alongside this work, I explore questions of epistemic agency, critical approaches to AI, and the ways systems can shape how people understand and navigate the possibilities available to them.

Research interests

My research interests include artificial intelligence, decision-making and epistemic agency; AI governance and regulation; international law; institutions and political systems; migration and human mobility; international affairs and diplomacy; Latin America; and critical approaches to artificial intelligence.
