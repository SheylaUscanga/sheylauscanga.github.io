Professional Experience

My professional experience has brought me into contact with migration, consular affairs, electoral processes, international affairs, and youth programs. Across these settings, I have worked at the intersection of institutions, public-facing processes, and international contexts.

Work within a university-community initiative focused on migration, displacement, and intercultural engagement. My responsibilities have included monitoring, organizing, and classifying mobility-related files, supporting documentation processes, and assisting with legal-orientation protocols for people in mobility situations.

I have also supported university students with migration and adaptation processes, contributed to the documentation of three intercultural initiatives, and assisted with events connecting public institutions and universities.

Experience includes multilingual academic support for students in mobility situations in Aguascalientes and San Luis Potosí, as well as operational support for a three-day mobility event with more than 400 attendees.

Supported activities related to consular assistance and protection, administrative procedures, and documentation concerning the rights of people in mobility situations outside Mexican territory.

The work involved public-facing assistance, document management, and administrative coordination for consular procedures, including cases involving repatriation and the transmission of official documentation through the office.

Approximately 10 Mexican nationals and 5 international visitors were assisted per day during a six-month period.

Participated in the 2025 electoral process for the election of members of the Mexican Judiciary, supporting operational activities related to electoral information and voter data.

Coordinated a team working with a newly implemented system for the control and capture of voter data, helping organize information and maintain accuracy throughout the process.

Member of Delegación Minerva, a BUAP student delegation focused on preparation and participation in Model United Nations. The delegation includes internal preparation classes and participation in several international simulation conferences.

Through these activities, I have worked on research, public speaking, negotiation, diplomatic representation, and multilateral procedure. I have represented Mexico, France, and Chile in committees addressing international security and legal cooperation.

Work has included developing more than eight proposals concerning international legal structures and conducting comparative research on migration and asylum legislation through a simulation involving delegations from ten countries.

Worked in an international summer camp program in New Mexico, supporting youth activities in a multicultural environment and contributing to the daily operation of the camp.

Responsibilities included sports activities and youth programming, working with participants from different cultural and linguistic backgrounds.
